# Canva free plan — the four walls and the workarounds (from the ToolRadar test)

Measured live on a free Canva account, November 2026. Every workaround below is what
was actually used in the video to finish the kit for $0.

## Wall 1 — Brand Kit stops at 3 colors
Free gets 1 Brand Kit with 3 colors (pricing page). Adding a 4th color is refused —
the button becomes an upgrade prompt.
Workaround: keep the 3 most-used colors in the kit and type the remaining hex codes
in by hand (color picker → enter hex). Slower, same result.

## Wall 2 — Pro photos carry a watermark, clean download is blocked
A crown-marked photo shows a watermark grid in the editor, and downloading a clean
copy is blocked: the choices are buying a license for that one image or downloading
a watermarked draft for free.
Workaround: swap the Pro photo for a free one (filter/search for unmarked photos and
zoom in to check for the crown before you commit the layout). The poster in this kit
was rebuilt around a free photo.

## Wall 3 — Background remover is Pro
Clicking it on a free account opens the upgrade offer instead of removing anything.
Workaround: design so you never need it — use full-bleed photo blocks and solid
brand-color panels behind text instead of cut-out subjects.

## Wall 4 — Resize is Pro
"Resize" and custom-size conversion both sit behind the upgrade button on free.
Workaround: create each size as its own design from the start (1080×1080 post,
1080×1920 Story, A5 flyer) and rebuild the layout by hand. The Story rebuild took
about 8 minutes once the layout was decided.

## The AI meter — the tightest wall
The free account shows only a percentage bar, never a number. Counted live until it
stopped: 24 successful uses (15 Magic Write text generations + 9 image generations,
4 images each). The 25th use was refused; the meter resets on the 1st of the month.
Image generations cost visibly more of the bar than text. Plan AI use accordingly:
write all your copy first, generate images last, and don't browse-generate.
